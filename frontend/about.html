🌿 About NutriWise AI

NutriWise AI is an AI-powered nutrition assistant that helps users analyze food, understand nutrition labels, and make healthier food choices through intelligent recommendations.

Problem Statement

Many people struggle to understand nutrition labels and often make unhealthy food choices because nutrition information is difficult to interpret. This can lead to poor dietary habits and health issues.

Our Solution

NutriWise AI uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze food images and nutrition labels, providing users with health scores, nutritional information, healthier alternatives, and personalized recommendations in a simple and user-friendly way.

🤖 AI Features

Food Image Analysis

Upload a food image and let AI identify the food item and analyze its nutritional value.

Health Score

Get an easy-to-understand health score based on calories, protein, fats, sugar, and other nutrients.

Smart Recommendations

Receive healthier alternatives and personalized nutrition suggestions.

History Tracking

Review your previous food scans and monitor your eating habits over time.
